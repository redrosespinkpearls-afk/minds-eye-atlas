Participant privacy · Cloudflare edition

What happens to your answers?

Before you choose to contribute

Your answers and personal scores are calculated in your browser. Opening and completing the quiz does not automatically publish a result. You can view the collective chart without contributing.

If you choose to contribute

  • Your browser sends your 12 imagery ratings, 12 feature responses, 15 challenge answers, and active-response times over HTTPS to this website's Cloudflare Worker to validate and recalculate your score.
  • The Worker saves only summary scores, visualization category, processing strategy, submission timestamp, public-list choice, and a hashed deletion token in a private Cloudflare D1 database.
  • The Worker does not save raw answers or timing arrays in the database and this project contains no application logging of them. Cloudflare as an infrastructure provider may still process network metadata.
  • A public circular chart includes your summary in the count of consenting respondents.
  • Your individual summary and optional display name are listed publicly only if you separately check the individual-listing box. The box is off by default.

Deletion and control

After submitting, use Copy private deletion code on the collective page. The code lets you remove the matching summary from the database at any time, including from another device. The database stores a cryptographic hash of that code, not its plaintext. If you lose the code and clear your browser, the website cannot automatically identify which anonymous submission was yours.

Contributions remain stored until deleted or until the website operator removes them. There is no automatic retention period in this version.

What is not collected by this project

This site does not ask for an email, address, birth date, or account login. No advertising pixels, analytics SDKs, or third-party tracking scripts are included. The project does not deliberately store visitor IP addresses in D1. Your browser and Cloudflare may still process technical connection data as part of web delivery.

Scientific limitations

This is an exploratory questionnaire, not a validated medical diagnosis or intelligence test. Category cutoffs and response-time reference windows are custom, and self-selected survey results cannot establish population prevalence.

Database owner: the person who deploys this Worker into their Cloudflare account. This policy describes the code shipped with the Mind's Eye Atlas Cloudflare package; the owner should review their hosting and privacy obligations before inviting the public.
